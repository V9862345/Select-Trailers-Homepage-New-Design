Frequently asked questions

What types of trailers does Select Trailer Co. sell?

We sell horse trailers, living quarter trailers, livestock and lo-pro livestock trailers, toy haulers, cargo trailers and car/racing trailers from brands including Delta, Lakota, Exiss, Sooner, Big Bend and Cimarron.

Where is Select Trailer Co. located?

We are at 3181 US 231 North, Shelbyville, TN 37160. Call (931) 685-4040 for directions or to confirm availability before you visit.

Do you offer trailer financing?

Yes. We offer low down payments on select models with approved credit and simple-interest loans with terms up to 20 years through trusted lenders.

Can I customize my horse trailer?

Yes. Many models can be personalized with options to match your lifestyle and budget. Our team will walk you through what is available.

Do you provide parts and service?

Yes. Our parts and service department handles repairs, maintenance and upgrades so you can buy and service your trailer in one place.

What are your business hours?

Sales: Mon–Fri 9:00 AM–5:00 PM. Parts & Service: Mon–Thu 8:00 AM–5:30 PM, Friday 8:00 AM–5:00 PM.
